Who We Are

Not Your Usual Caterer.

Leafy Flavours is a boutique food experience service based in Kellyville, Sydney. We specialise in authentic Mysuru, Karnataka and Sanketi cuisine, and Indian street food done properly, styled and served at your venue.

We are not a bulk caterer. We don't do pickup trays. We arrive at your home or venue, dress the table to your theme, and set everything up ready to serve. Then we step back and leave you to your occasion. What stays behind is the memory of a spread that felt like it actually meant something.

Because Karnataka food deserves more than a disposable container.

Our Story

It Started with a Habbada Oota at Home.

Leafy Flavours began in 2023 as Sydney's first Karnataka supper club, run from our own kitchen in Kellyville. What started as festival suppers for a handful of guests now travels to homes right across Sydney.

We love our culture and our festivals, and this is how we share them with Sydney. We pour our heart into every event as if it were our own, because for us it genuinely is.
