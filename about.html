Meet the Owners

The Two of Us.

In 2023 we began hosting Karnataka festival supper clubs in our home in Kellyville. Sydney had plenty of South Indian food, but not this: the habbada oota, the meal that belongs to a particular festival, served the way it is served back home.

Each supper club was built around a festival. The dishes that appear only for that occasion, the reason they appear, and the stories that come with them. Not just a menu, but why the food looks like this on this particular day.

We are Sanketis, a small community from Karnataka, and part of what we set out to do was give our heritage dishes the spotlight they rarely get. Some of these recipes live in very few kitchens outside our own families.

How It Grew

Sydney’s First Karnataka Supper Club.

Those early supper clubs were traditional sit down Elle Oota. Guests seated, courses served in order, exactly as we would at home. Nothing about it was designed for Sydney. That was rather the point.

People started talking about it. Then they started asking whether we could bring that same table to their place instead, for a naming ceremony, a housewarming, a birthday.

So we did. What began as festival supper clubs grew into grazing tables, sit down feasts and festive curations across Sydney. The scale never changed on purpose. We still take only a couple of events a month, because we still cook every one of them ourselves.

We are not a full time catering company.

Both of us work full time in IT. Leafy Flavours is our passion project, cooked on weekends, around our jobs and our home. That is exactly why we take so few events. Every booking is one we chose to say yes to, and one we have the time to do properly.

We love our culture and our festivals, and this is how we share them with Sydney. We pour our heart into every event as if it were our own, because for us it genuinely is. Deepika & Chetan  ·  Kellyville, Sydney
